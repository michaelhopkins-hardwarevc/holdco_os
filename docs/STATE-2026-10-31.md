## The short version

The build is ahead of the adoption. Phase 1 of the spec (§7.1 to §7.7) is
finished and deployed, the capture to Xero loop is proven end-to-end on real
data, and the suite is green at 261 tests across 39 files. What has not happened
is use: in the eight weeks the loop has been live, three blocks of time reached
approved or invoiced (1.75 hours) and one real invoice reached Xero at $250.
Confirmation stopped on September 28.

The cause is the one the operator named on October 30: the confirm step asks a
founder a delivery person's question. The morning-read scope is the correction,
and none of it is built.

## What is built and deployed

- **Project accounting core (§7.1 to §7.7).** Entities, invitations, four roles,
  RLS default-deny through `membership`; clients, projects, phases, resources,
  indirect codes; weekly timesheet grid plus single entry, submit and approve;
  expenses with receipts in private storage; draft invoices from approved
  uninvoiced time and expenses, WIP, AR aging, branded PDF; project
  profitability, utilization, firm dashboard, CSV on every list; CSV import
  mapped to the interim workbook.
- **The automation spine (WIS M0 to M4).** Nightly capture from each connected
  mailbox (sent mail and calendar), Monday and HubSpot, read-only; crosswalks
  plus explicit per-source rules (project, indirect, ignore); drafting with real
  meeting duration, a 12 hour daily ceiling, an evidence line and a confidence
  label; confirming is approving; Queue with one-tap mapping that teaches a rule
  and refiles matching events; the `/not-tracked` contract page.
- **Xero wiring.** OAuth per entity with encrypted tokens and refresh; draft
  invoice push behind a reconciliation guard; project tagging through the free
  `Project` tracking category (34 live options named code plus project name,
  all 7 app projects crosswalked).

## The metric that settles it

The morning read is the product bet, so instrument it. The share of yesterday's
proposed hours confirmed, per person, per day, shown on the screen from
milestone 4 onward. Sustained above roughly 80 percent with one tap and the
system has replaced Ajera. Below it, narrow the app to delivery billable time,
where the day genuinely is a timesheet, and keep the founder day out.
